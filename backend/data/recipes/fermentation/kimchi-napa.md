## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Napa cabbage | 1000g | 100% |
| Coarse sea salt | 50g | 5% |
| Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) | 60g | 6% |
| Garlic, minced | 30g | 3% |
| Ginger, minced | 10g | 1% |
| Sugar | 20g | 2% |
| Soy sauce | 30g | 3% |
| Scallions, chopped | 50g | 5% |

## Method

Cut the napa cabbage into quarters and rub the coarse sea salt between the leaves. Let the cabbage sit for 2 hours, turning once, until the leaves are pliable, then rinse thoroughly under cold water and drain well. Make a paste from the gochugaru, garlic, ginger, sugar, and soy sauce. Massage the paste and scallions into the salted cabbage, working it into every leaf while wearing gloves. Pack the seasoned cabbage tightly into a clean jar, pressing down until brine rises above the solids. Leave the jar at room temperature for 3-5 days, pressing daily to release gas, then move it to the refrigerator once it reaches the desired tang.

## Allergen Note

Contains soy (soy sauce). No shellfish or fish sauce used in this vegan recipe.
