## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Whole milk | 1000g | 100% |
| Live yogurt starter culture | 50g | 5% |

## Method

Heat the whole milk to 85C, stirring occasionally to prevent scorching, and hold it at that temperature for 5 minutes to denature the proteins. Cool the milk down to 43C, then whisk in the live yogurt starter culture until fully combined. Pour the inoculated milk into a clean container, cover, and incubate at a steady 43C for 6-8 hours until set with a gentle wobble. Chill the yogurt in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours before serving to firm it up further.

## Allergen Note

Contains dairy (milk). Not suitable for vegans.
