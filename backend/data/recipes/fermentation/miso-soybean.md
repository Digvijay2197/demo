title: Homemade Soybean Miso Paste
cuisine: Japanese
dietary_tags: vegan, gluten-free

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Dried soybeans | 500g | 100% |
| Koji rice | 500g | 100% |
| Sea salt | 150g | 30% |
| Reserved bean cooking water | 100g | 20% |

## Method

Soak the dried soybeans overnight in plenty of water, then drain and simmer them for 3-4 hours until they mash easily between two fingers, reserving some of the cooking water. Mash the warm soybeans until mostly smooth, then mix in the koji rice and sea salt, adding reserved cooking water a little at a time until the mixture holds together like a firm paste. Pack the mixture tightly into a fermentation crock, pressing out air pockets, and smooth the surface. Weigh the paste down with a clean weight, cover, and ferment in a cool, dark place for 6-12 months, checking occasionally for surface mold to remove.

## Allergen Note

Contains soy. Produced with koji (Aspergillus oryzae cultured rice); no gluten-containing grains used.
