title: Sweet Kombucha with Ginger Second Ferment
cuisine: American / Fermented Beverage
dietary_tags: vegan, gluten-free

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Filtered water | 1000g | 100% |
| Black tea leaves | 10g | 1% |
| White sugar | 100g | 10% |
| SCOBY | 1 piece | - |
| Starter kombucha liquid | 100g | 10% |
| Fresh ginger, sliced (second ferment) | 20g | 2% |

## Method

Bring the filtered water to a boil and steep the black tea leaves for 10 minutes, then remove the leaves and dissolve the white sugar into the hot tea. Allow the sweet tea to cool completely to room temperature before pouring it into a fermentation vessel. Add the SCOBY and starter kombucha liquid, cover with a breathable cloth, and ferment at room temperature for 7-10 days until pleasantly tart. Strain the kombucha into bottles, add the sliced fresh ginger to each bottle, seal, and leave for a second ferment of 2-3 days at room temperature to build carbonation before refrigerating.

## Allergen Note

Contains caffeine from black tea. No common food allergens present.
