## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Green cabbage | 1500g | 100% |
| Sea salt | 30g | 2% |
| Caraway seeds | 5g | 0.33% |

## Method

Shred the green cabbage finely and place it in a large bowl with the sea salt and caraway seeds. Massage and squeeze the cabbage by hand for 8-10 minutes until it releases enough liquid to form a brine. Pack the cabbage tightly into a fermentation jar, pressing it down so the brine fully covers the solids, and weigh it down with a fermentation weight. Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for 2-4 weeks, burping the jar daily to release fermentation gas and skimming any surface scum. Taste periodically after week two and refrigerate once the sourness reaches the desired level.
